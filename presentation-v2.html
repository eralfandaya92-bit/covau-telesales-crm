Operating Model

The Life of a Lead

The CRM follows the lead through the complete telesales journey — not just a spreadsheet row.

01NewFresh or newly assigned lead.
02In ProgressAgent begins contact attempts.
03ContactedCustomer contact is established.
04NurturingFollow-up and communication continue.
05OpportunityQualification → analysis → proposal → negotiation.
06ConvertedOnly Closed Won counts as converted.

↻ For Recycling

Eligible unsuccessful leads retain their history and can be reassigned according to approved recycling rules.

⊘ For Disposal

Non-recyclable outcomes leave the active sales journey according to approved business policy.

Agent Experience

A Clear Next Action on Every Lead

1. Prioritise

Agent opens the work queue and sees the lead, priority, stage and follow-up context.

2. Engage

Call, email or SMS from the customer record while communication remains attached to the Lead ID.

3. Progress

Record disposition, decision maker, follow-up, invoice, tariff, quote and opportunity stage.
Target workflow: Open Lead → Contact → Record Outcome → Schedule Follow-up → Analyse Account → Update Opportunity → Save & Next Lead.
